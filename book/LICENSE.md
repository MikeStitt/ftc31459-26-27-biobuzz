## Why those terms and not looser ones

The guide adapts prose from Game Manual 0, which is licensed **CC BY-NC 2.0 Generic**
(<https://gm0.org/en/latest/docs/appendix/license.html>, read 2026-09-27). CC BY-NC 4.0 keeps every
restriction that license asks about the material taken from it: attribution, and non-commercial use.
Choosing terms at least as tight as the source's means no reading of what CC BY-NC 2.0 does and does
not require of an adaptation has to be right.

What has not been established: whether looser terms would be permitted. Section 4 of the CC BY-NC
2.0 legal code was read on 2026-09-27 and carries no sentence naming the license a derivative work
must use. That is not the same as establishing that any license would do, and nothing here relies on
it.
